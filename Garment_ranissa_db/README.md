# Ranissa ERP

## Overview

Ranissa ERP is a cloud-first Garment Manufacturing ERP built on **Supabase (PostgreSQL)**.

The system is designed specifically for apparel manufacturers and covers the entire business lifecycle from raw material purchasing to production, inventory, costing and sales.

---

## Features

- Authentication & Role Based Access
- Company Management
- Customer Management
- Supplier Management
- Fabric & Trim Masters
- Style Management
- Bill of Materials (BOM)
- Purchase Orders
- Sales Orders
- Inventory Management
- Production Planning
- Cost Sheet
- Reports
- Power BI Integration
